# 0006 - No NBA marks in fanwire's own promotion

Decision: 2026-11-03, the human, after the IP research now in
`wiki/CodeContext/FrontendUI/branding.md` §10.

## Context

fanwire shows NBA statistics and names teams and players, and since
`FRONTEND-031` it shows each team as its abbreviation on the team's two
colours. Facts and referential mentions inside the app are low risk. The
exposure is implied affiliation: a team or league mark used in advertising a
service is "use" under Trademarks Act s.4, which opens passing off (s.7(b))
and depreciation of goodwill (s.22). The NBA's licensing agent, NBA
Properties, polices its marks.

## Choice

fanwire's own promotion never directly advertises an NBA team, in any
capacity. No ad, social media post, app-store listing or screenshot, or promo
graphic made by or for fanwire carries a team or league name, abbreviation,
logo, colour pair or team icon, nor a player's name or image.

This binds fanwire, not its users. Users may name teams and players in their
posts; the terms of service handle user conduct instead (`branding.md` §10).

## Consequences

- Marketing material describes fanwire generically: a sports social app with
  basketball stats.
- App-store screenshots and promo images use views without team icons or
  names, or fixture data with invented teams.
- In-app use (stats, team icons, mentions) is unaffected.
